    <h2 id="what-is-gearbuddy">What is GearBuddy?</h2>
    <p>GearBuddy is an independent, research-based guide site — not a store. We don't sell products, hold inventory, or manufacture anything. Our sole purpose is to help US shoppers navigate the crowded, often confusing market for ergonomic desk gear.</p>
    <p>We focus on the US market and products that are readily available on Amazon, compiling specifications and public user reviews so you can make an informed decision before purchasing.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="how-we-research">
    <h2 id="how-we-research">How we research</h2>
    <p>Transparency matters to us, so here's exactly how we build our guides:</p>

    <h3>Manufacturer specifications</h3>
    <p>We start with official product pages, spec sheets, and documentation released by manufacturers. This gives us an objective, verifiable baseline — dimensions, materials, sensor specs, battery life figures, switch ratings, charging wattages, and more. We cross-reference these across multiple sources where possible.</p>

    <h3>Amazon User Reviews</h3>
    <p>We evaluate publicly available Amazon reviews focusing on verified purchases. We look for patterns: recurring praise, recurring complaints, reports of long-term reliability or premature failure. A product with 4.5 stars from 10,000 reviews tells a very different story than one with 4.5 stars from 50 reviews.</p>

    <h3>Community Discussions &amp; Forums</h3>
    <p>We monitor and research relevant communities including:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://reddit.com/r/MechanicalKeyboards" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">r/MechanicalKeyboards</a> — in-depth keyboard comparisons, switch discussions, and long-term ownership reports</li>
      <li><a href="https://reddit.com/r/ErgoMechKeyboards" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">r/ErgoMechKeyboards</a> — ergonomic keyboard layouts and split keyboard discussions</li>
      <li><a href="https://reddit.com/r/buildapc" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">r/buildapc</a> — broad community feedback on peripherals and desk setups</li>
      <li>Other relevant subreddits for mice, monitors, and desk accessories</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Community discussions often surface issues that don't appear in short-term reviews — build quality problems that emerge after months of use, compatibility quirks, firmware issues, and comparisons against competing products made by people who've owned both.</p>

    <div class="callout callout--warning" role="note">
      <strong>Important:</strong> GearBuddy does <strong>not</strong> personally test, use, or receive products for review. All of our recommendations are based entirely on publicly available information — manufacturer specifications and aggregated public user feedback. We always encourage you to read recent reviews yourself and to consider your own needs before purchasing.
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="how-we-pick-winners">
    <h2 id="how-we-pick-winners">How we pick winners</h2>
    <p>When evaluating products for a guide, we apply a consistent set of criteria:</p>

    <ul class="criteria-list">
      <li>
        <strong>Ergonomic Design Principles</strong> — Does the product's design align with established ergonomic guidelines? For mice, this means promoting a neutral wrist position. For keyboards, it means layout choices that reduce finger travel and wrist deviation. We use publicly available ergonomic research and guidelines as reference points.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Build Quality Reputation</strong> — We assess long-term durability based on aggregated user reports. A product with consistent complaints about cable fraying, switch chatter, or premature failure won't make our top picks regardless of its spec sheet.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Value for Money</strong> — We consider what you're getting relative to the price. A premium keyboard needs to offer meaningfully more than an entry-level alternative to earn a recommendation at that tier. We always try to include budget and premium options.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>User Satisfaction Signals</strong> — Overall review scores, ratio of positive to negative reviews, specific praise for features relevant to our criteria, and the quality and volume of community endorsements all factor into our assessment.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Compatibility with Common Setups</strong> — We prioritize products that work well with standard desk configurations — Windows and macOS compatibility, standard connectivity options, easy setup, and minimal required software.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </section>

    <h2 id="price-methodology">Price history and discount tracking methodology</h2>
    <p>A major tenet of our editorial guidelines is protecting shoppers from manufactured discounts and misleading "list price" inflation. We do not display dynamic or real-time prices on our pages to ensure compliance with Amazon Associates operating standards, but our product evaluation process incorporates rigorous price history verification:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Baseline tracking:</strong> We monitor rolling 90-day and 180-day price trends across Amazon.com via independent price-history trackers like CamelCamelCamel to verify whether an advertised discount represents genuine value.</li>
      <li><strong>MSRP sanity checks:</strong> Manufacturers occasionally elevate the strike-through "List Price" on Amazon right before major promotional periods like Prime Day or Black Friday. We cross-reference manufacturer direct store pricing to confirm actual historical selling averages.</li>
      <li><strong>Value tier evaluation:</strong> We classify peripherals into entry-level, midrange, and premium tiers based on consistent historical pricing brackets rather than temporary flash-sale anomalies.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
